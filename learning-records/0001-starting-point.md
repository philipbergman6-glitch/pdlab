# Starting point: strong math, zero game theory

Philip (2026-08-30) reports strong math — linear algebra, ODEs, proofs — but no prior game theory at all. Future lessons can use eigenvalues, Jacobians, geometric series and formal proofs without explanation, but every game-theoretic term (strategy, equilibrium, dominance, subgame, discount factor) must be introduced explicitly the first time.

**Implications:** Compress the math, expand the concepts. Timeline is a couple of days → one concept per lesson, ~15 min each, eight lessons total (see NOTES.md).
