# Prisoner's Dilemma Resources

URLs verified 2026-08-30 by a research subagent (fetched and matched) unless marked (UNVERIFIED).

## Knowledge

- [Encyclopedia: _Prisoner's Dilemma_ — Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy (Kuhn)](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/prisoner-dilemma/)
  One-shot, finite/infinite iteration, noise, evolution, spatial PD, zero-determinant. Use for: conceptual backbone, citation-grade definitions. Primary source for Lesson 1.
- [Lecture notes: _14.12 Economic Applications of Game Theory (Fall 2025)_ — MIT OCW](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/14-12-economic-applications-of-game-theory-fall-2025/lists/lecture-notes/)
  Free PDFs: dominance, rationalizability, NE, SPNE, repeated games. Use for: rigorous one-shot proofs and discounting derivations (Lessons 1–4).
- [Textbook (free PDF w/ registration): _A Course in Game Theory_ — Osborne & Rubinstein, 1994](https://books.osborne.economics.utoronto.ca/)
  Ch. 8 repeated games: Folk theorems, trigger strategies. Use for: formal statements/proofs (Lesson 4).
- [Video course: _ECON 159 Game Theory_ — Ben Polak, Open Yale](https://oyc.yale.edu/economics/econ-159)
  24 lectures + transcripts; discount thresholds worked numerically. Use for: intuition before formalizing.
- [Paper: _Effective Choice in the Prisoner's Dilemma_ (JCR 1980) — Axelrod](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/002200278002400101)
  First tournament; TFT wins. Paywalled. Use for: primary tournament citation (Lesson 5).
- [Paper (free PDF): _The Evolution of Cooperation_ (Science 1981) — Axelrod & Hamilton](https://websites.umich.edu/~axe/research/Axelrod%20and%20Hamilton%20EC%201981.pdf)
  Tournament + ESS of TFT, shadow-of-the-future threshold w. Use for: linking tournaments to δ*/ESS (Lessons 4–6).
- [Paper (free PDF mirror): _The Folk Theorem in Repeated Games with Discounting…_ (Econometrica 1986) — Fudenberg & Maskin](http://dklevine.com/archive/refs4224.pdf)
  Discounted SPE folk theorem. JSTOR: https://www.jstor.org/stable/1911307. Use for: canonical Folk Theorem citation (Lesson 4).
- [Paper: _Evolutionary games and spatial chaos_ (Nature 1992) — Nowak & May](https://www.nature.com/articles/359826a0)
  Lattice PD, clustering sustains cooperation. Paywalled abstract. Use for: spatial model spec (Lesson 7).
- [Paper (free): _Iterated PD contains strategies that dominate any evolutionary opponent_ (PNAS 2012) — Press & Dyson](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3387070/)
  Zero-determinant strategies, extortion. Use for: ZD derivation (Lesson 8).
- [Paper (free): _Five rules for the evolution of cooperation_ (Science 2006) — Nowak](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3279745/)
  b/c threshold rules for five mechanisms. Use for: unifying frame for evolutionary/spatial results.
- [Survey (free PDF): _Evolutionary Game Dynamics_ (Bull. AMS 2003) — Hofbauer & Sigmund](https://www.ams.org/journals/bull/2003-40-04/S0273-0979-03-00988-1/S0273-0979-03-00988-1.pdf)
  Replicator equation, ESS, NE-vs-rest-point theorems. Use for: correct theorem statements (Lesson 6).
- [Lecture notes (free PDF): _Games and Dynamics_ — van Strien, Imperial (2015)](https://www.ma.imperial.ac.uk/~dturaev/gamesdynamics.pdf)
  Replicator dynamics, ESS with proofs. Use for: worked math, Lesson 6.
- [Library docs: _Axelrod-Python_](https://axelrod.readthedocs.io/en/stable/)
  230+ strategies, noisy/spatial tournaments, Moran process. Use for: reproducible simulations in the project.
- [Interactive: _The Evolution of Trust_ — Nicky Case](https://ncase.me/trust/)
  Playable repeated-PD explainer. Use for: intuition and demo inspiration; not a citation.
